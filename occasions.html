Occasions

We take about thirty a year, and we turn down more than that.

Weddings, dinners and the occasional company that understands what it is booking. The estate is not an events venue with a house attached; it is a house that will, carefully, hold an event.

Exclusive use of the house and gardens from £14,000 for a full day. The Tithe Barn alone starts at £4,200. Both include the estate team, and neither includes catering, which goes through our own kitchen.

Before you ask

Three things we will not do.

  • Fireworks — never. There are 340 veteran oaks and a barn owl population here.
  • Marquees on the south lawn — the ground is a scheduled archaeological site. The Tithe Barn exists precisely so you do not need one.
  • Amplified music outdoors after 22:00 — the estate is a working farm with neighbours who calve at four in the morning.
  • Two weddings in a weekend — one at a time, so the gardens get a day to recover.
