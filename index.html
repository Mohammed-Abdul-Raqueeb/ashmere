The estate

Held in trust, worked as a farm, open most days.

Ashmere has been in one family since 1583 and in a charitable trust since 2011. The trust owns the house, the park and the endowment that keeps them; the farm pays its own way.

There is no theme, no trail and nothing costumed. The house is open five days a week in season, the gardens and the meadow are open every day of the year, and the best thing here costs nothing and is a field.

The long plan

Costed to 2124.

Every commitment we make is priced out to the end of the next century — the roof, the yew walk, the oaks, the meadow. Anything we cannot fund that far ahead, we do not start.

It sounds grand and it is mostly just arithmetic. It also means nobody here is in a hurry, which visitors notice within about ten minutes.
