Visit

The gardens are open every day of the year.

The house keeps season hours; everything outdoors does not. Nothing here needs booking except the house in high summer, and the meadow never costs anything.

Getting here

Twenty minutes off the motorway and then a lane.

  1. By car

    Twenty minutes from the M40, then two miles of single-track lane with passing places. Free parking in the meadow field, or on hard standing when it is wet.

  2. By train

    Nearest station is eleven minutes away by taxi. There are usually two waiting; if there are not, ring the estate office and we will sort something out.

  3. On foot

    The valley footpath runs through the park and is open whether we are or not. It is four miles from the village and the last mile is the good bit.

  4. By bicycle

    Racks at the stable yard. The lane is narrow and fast in places; the bridleway from the north is slower and much nicer.
